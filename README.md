## Ethan Bocquet

Engineering student at École Centrale de Lyon (general engineering programme, after CPGE MP2I–MPI*). I learnt algorithmics and low-level programming (C, OCaml) during preparatory classes and now build applied Python projects in **machine learning and data analysis**, with a growing focus on **AI and cybersecurity**.

### AI & Machine learning

**[Neural network from scratch](https://github.com/EthanBoc/neural-network-from-scratch)**
Multilayer perceptron with hand-written backpropagation in NumPy (regression and classification), verified by numerical gradient checking, plus a systematic hyper-parameter study.

**[Multivariate analysis & clustering](https://github.com/EthanBoc/multivariate-analysis-clustering)**
PCA and MCA implemented from the general factor-analysis formulation, combined with hierarchical clustering and k-means.

### Hackathons

**UNBOXED Hackathon** — GE HealthCare | February 2026
Design, in teams of four, of an AI agent to assist with radiological diagnosis, assessed through a comprehensive and functional workflow (segmentation → analysis → report generation), using medical image segmentation and a human validation loop with a radiologist.

**Fight the Fraud Hackathon** — Sopra Steria / AWS / Le Village by CA | October 2025
Development, in teams of four, of a simulator for detecting telephone fraud within 48 hours, assessed by the delivery of a functional full-stack application (voice verification + deepfake detection), whilst learning FastAPI, SpeechBrain, Hugging Face Transformers and React/TypeScript without any prior experience in ML.

### Other projects

**[Vélo'v Traffic Monitor](https://github.com/EthanBoc/velov-traffic-monitor)**: full-stack group project (4 students, 164 commits) analysing Lyon's bike-share network. Project lead and database design.

**[Optimal Color Palette](https://github.com/EthanBoc/optimal-color-palette)**: four from-scratch palette-selection methods for image quantization, benchmarked against Pillow.
